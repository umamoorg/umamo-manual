***************
Troubleshooting
***************

This chapter is for when something goes wrong: Umamo runs out of memory, a model doesn't open, a message appears that you do not understand, or you have found a bug and want to report it.

Where to Look
=============

.. list-table::
	:header-rows: 1

	* - What happened
	  - Read
	* - Umamo ran out of memory, or it is slow, or it crashed while working on a large model.
	  - :doc:`performance_and_memory`
	* - The installer is blocked, the window is blank or the viewport is empty, or something differs on your operating system.
	  - :doc:`platform_notes`
	* - Work was lost, a file will not open, or you want to know how Umamo protects your puppet files.
	  - :doc:`backups_and_data_safety`
	* - A notice, an alert, or an error appeared and you want to know what it means.
	  - :doc:`common_messages`
	* - You found a bug, or you need a file format or a feature that Umamo does not have.
	  - :doc:`reporting_bugs`

If unsure, open the log first.  Almost every problem leaves a line in it, and it is what a bug report needs.  See :doc:`reporting_bugs`.
